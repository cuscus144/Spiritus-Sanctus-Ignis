Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Divine Increase Business Network, a ministry of Spiritus Sanctus Ignis Ministry, collects from members and visitors, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who We Are

Divine Increase Business Network ("Divine Increase", "we", "us", "our") is a private business directory and community platform operated by Spiritus Sanctus Ignis Ministry for its members. Our registered address is 20 Uchemec Street Igboelerin, Ojo, Lagos. You can reach us at spiritussanctusignisministry@gmail.com or by phone at 09123597543 / 09160803602.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & profile information — your name, phone number, email address, business name, business category, description, and any photos or logos you upload.
  • Directory & advert content — details submitted when you list a business or apply to advertise, including payment-proof screenshots where advertising fees apply.
  • Wallet & giving activity — donation and wallet-funding confirmations you submit (amount, payment method, and proof of payment). We do not collect or store your card or bank account credentials.
  • Community activity — messages you post in Community Chat, testimonies, prayer requests, and event responses.
  • Device & usage information — basic technical data such as browser type and app usage, collected automatically to keep the platform secure and working properly.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your member account and business profile.
  • To list and display your business in the Directory and Featured Businesses.
  • To review and confirm wallet funding, donations, and advert payments.
  • To send notifications relevant to your account, such as approval updates or event reminders.
  • To keep the Divine Increase community safe, trustworthy, and reserved for verified ministry members.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. Business profile details you choose to publish (such as your business name, category, description, and contact details) are visible to other members in the Directory. Payment confirmations and proof of payment are reviewed by ministry administrators for verification purposes only. We may share limited information with trusted service providers (such as hosting and notification services) strictly to operate the platform, and only where required to do so by law.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account and directory information for as long as your membership is active, or as needed to provide the services described in this policy. You may request that your profile or account be removed at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Choices

  • You can review and edit your business profile at any time from your dashboard.
  • You can ask us to correct, update, or delete your personal information.
  • You can contact us with any privacy question or concern using the details above.

7. Children's Privacy

Divine Increase is intended for adult members of Spiritus Sanctus Ignis Ministry and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
